The gateway runs as two workloads: "{{ include "tabularia.fullname" . }}-gateway" answers
requests ({{ if .Values.gateway.autoscaling.enabled }}autoscaled{{ else }}{{ .Values.gateway.replicas }} replica(s){{ end }}; scale it with gateway.replicas when people wait) and
"{{ include "tabularia.fullname" . }}-orchestrator" fires the schedules and executes the flows
({{ .Values.gateway.orchestrator.replicas }} replica(s)). One component is single-replica BY DESIGN and the chart does
not let you scale it: beat (N clocks enqueue every periodic job N times).
